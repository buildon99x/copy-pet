## Reddit / indie maker post

**Title:** I built a clipboard manager that lives on your desktop as a cat

I made **ClipCat**, a free Windows clipboard manager with a tiny desktop cat companion.

When you copy something, your cat reacts. It quietly sleeps when idle, and you can pet it with a click. There are 12 cats to choose from.

It's a practical tool too: local text and image clipboard history, search, tags, pins, and quick access to previously copied items. On Windows, the default picker shortcut is Win+Shift+V (with Ctrl+Shift+V as the fallback if registration fails).

There's no account or cloud sync. I tried to keep the cat expressive without interrupting actual work.

I'd especially appreciate feedback on whether the character feels helpful or distracting during a real workday.

Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NZBR8X5T677
